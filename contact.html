Frequently Asked Questions

How does farm-to-table cold-chain work?

When you order perishable organic produce, the harvest is gathered at sunrise, pre-chilled in on-farm cooling stations, packed into compostable insulated pods with non-toxic freeze packs, and dispatched via express courier to arrive within 24-48 hours.

How do I verify a seller's organic credentials?

Every producer profile and product detail page displays verified badges linked directly to their USDA National Organic Program (NOP) certifier ID number and lab assay reports.

What is your freshness & return guarantee?

If any fresh produce or seeds arrive damaged or substandard, simply send a photo within 48 hours of delivery for an instant replacement or 100% refund without return shipping hassle.
