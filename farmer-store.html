Generations of Regenerative Heritage

Robert MacIntyre represents the third generation of farmers tending the loam soils of Willamette Valley. In 2012, the entire acreage transitioned away from synthetic fertilizers to 100% biological compost tea injections, cover cropping with hairy vetch and crimson clover, and rotational livestock grazing.

Soil Health & Traceability

All seeds are preserved in non-hybrid heirloom libraries and tested annually for 0.00% glyphosate or synthetic residue. Harvests are gathered early at morning dew and packed directly into biodegradable shipping units within 3 hours.
